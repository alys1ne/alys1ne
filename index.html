001 — The Project

She didn't have a voice.
Now she has a film.

ALYS1NE is an AI-driven cinematographic project born from a real story. A story of a young woman trapped in cycles of abuse, exploitation, and institutional violence — where every system that should have protected her became another wall.

Using generative AI as a filmmaking tool — not a gimmick — ALYS1NE transforms testimony into cinema. Every frame is constructed with the precision of traditional post-production and the visual language of film noir: black marble, gold light, no warmth, no comfort.

The project operates at the intersection of documentary truth and cinematic fiction. The story is real. The images are generated. The impact is undeniable.

"C'était son empire. Et elle n'y était que de passage."

004 — The Filmmaker

Behind the lens.

Ronny Nollet

Director · Editor · Creator of ALYS1NE

I've spent over 35 years in post-production for broadcast television, documentary, and narrative film. I've cut hundreds of hours of prime-time television across Belgium's major networks — VTM, VRT, Vier, Streamz — before turning my craft toward AI-driven cinema.

ALYS1NE is not a technological experiment. It is the work of a seasoned editor who understands rhythm, tension, and the weight of a single frame — now wielding new tools to tell stories that could not exist otherwise.

Based in Antwerp, Belgium. Working through Rest In Peace BV.
